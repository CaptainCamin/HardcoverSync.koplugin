## Still open

- **Compact button heights.** Used so far: 56 for a primary or full-width button, 40 for a small one (Sync now, Read more), 64 in sheets and dialogs; touch areas are never under 48. Not signed off.
- **Overlay rule order.** The 2 white band sits above the 3 black rule in every overlay; unchecked against the appendix.
- **Lato on a device.** Medium vs Regular, and whether the plugin can write to KOReader's fonts folder on a Kobo and a Kindle.
- **The Lists tab's rows** (the shelf icon, "7 books · ranked") have no mock of their own.

## Known deviations from the rules above

- Shelf screen: the sort popover lists all eleven orders (MMD says five or six); it fits a 600x800 screen. Mock 3's search icon is not built (there is no search within a shelf). The ranked vibe list keeps covers beside the numeral (mock 1d).

- Book details: Open only finds books the plugin has linked to a file (history, not the folder tree), so a book never opened here shows Find on device; the stats strip (rating, readers, reviews) is gone, and the author, the series and the status pill are not tappable (no search or shelf from the page; the owner's call); the figures are on the Reviews screen. The page lists three genres; moods and content warnings are only behind "+N more".

- Goals and Stats: "30 days" is not a Stats period (the data model has all time or one year; mock 8 shows it). The highlighted column is the best month, not the current one (mock 8). The Past goals tab's rows have no chip.

- Hold-only actions: unlink a book (`hardcover_menu.lua`, the "Linked book" row). Also holding the scroll bar's up or down triangle, which jumps to the start or end of the screen (`components/scroll_control.lua`): a long screen needs a fast way to either end and the triangles are the only control, so the owner accepts it as an exception with no visible control. Discard queued changes is a button in the Pending changes list now (`pending_changes_dialog.lua`); it was a hold on Sync.
- Grey fills and lines in the charts: levels down to 0xBB, a 0xD8 gridline, 0x88 dashes (`chart_widgets.lua`, `charts.lua`). Allowed (see the decisions), to be polished.
- `Theme.hatchRect` paints at 40% opacity, so its stripes anti-alias to grey; nothing calls it yet.
- Search: `components/search_field.lua` is the field (Home, the query on the results screen, and the two pickers, "Select book" and "Select edition"). Tapping it opens `components/search_bar.lua`, the top bar turned into a field (back arrow, pill with a clear X, KOReader's keyboard under it, Enter searches); the field on screen is not typed into itself. Departures from MMD: no Search button (the keyboard's Enter is the control) and the empty-field hint is KOReader's own grey, not black. Mock 3's search icon on a shelf is still not built. A picker's field has no clear X: a picker has to end in a choice.
- The pickers are not in the mocks. They reuse the shelf screen with a radio at the right end for the chosen row (filled, as in the choice sheets: no state by grey alone), and no chevron, since a tap chooses rather than opens. The edition's second line is its format and pages, because the title and author are the same for every edition.
- KOReader's own widgets are still used only for typing (the text field and keyboard inside the input dialog). The journal entry is a form of rows like the goal form, its text written in the input dialog. Numbers are set with the stepper dialog and dates with the date picker; everything else is on the new components.

## Prototypes

The approved whole-UI mockup is `docs/redesign/index.html`; the build is judged against it screen by screen. Where the build and the mockup differ on purpose, the decisions table above says so; where they differ by accident, the build is wrong.

Throwaway comparison scenes for the headless emulator (`spec/emu/scenarios/proto_*.lua`) live on branch
`claude/mmd-prototypes`. They are for deciding, not shipping. The emulator cannot show ghosting or refresh
latency; finalists need a look on a real Kobo or Kindle.

## Refreshing overlays

A dialog or sheet refreshes the full width of the screen across its height (and a few pixels more), with the same fast "ui" waveform as KOReader's own boxes. Refreshing exactly its box lost the right edge of a dialog on a real panel until the next full redraw. A toast keeps its exact box. The refresh is queued when the overlay closes, whoever closes it (`UIManager:close(picker)` as well as `overlay:close()`), as KOReader's own widgets do in `onCloseWidget`; a sheet closed without one stayed on the panel until something else redrew that area. A dialog that redraws as it changes (the progress dialog, the date picker) shows the new box first and closes the old one without a refresh (`Overlay:close(new)`), so a change is one refresh of the box, not two.
