Hardcover Sync: full redesign

Ten screens at the real canvas (1072 x 1448, authored at 572 x 772 design px, 1 u = 1.875 px) using the MMD appendix metrics: top bar 67 + 3px rule, nav bar 57, list rows with Black 21 / Medium 18 labels, 48 x 30 switches, 2px chips, 3 + 2 overlay rule, slim scroll bar with bare triangles. Everything is black and white except secondary text and chart fills, which may use grey (decided). The secondary-text colour is the proposed beta setting, flip it above to compare. This is a mockup in a browser, not a render from the plugin, so spacing is design intent and unverified on a panel.
